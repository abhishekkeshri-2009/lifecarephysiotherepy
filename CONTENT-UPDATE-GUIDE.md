# Content Update Guide

- Update contact and area details in `src/config/business.ts`.
- Update service copy in `src/content/services.ts`.
- Update condition summaries in `src/content/conditions.ts`.
- Update FAQs in `src/content/faqs.ts`.
- Update blog content in `src/content/blog.ts`.
- Replace placeholder imagery or add licensed assets in `public/`.
